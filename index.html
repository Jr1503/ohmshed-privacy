Ohm Shed privacy policy

Last updated 13 October 2026 · RealOps Connect, Saskatchewan, Canada

Ohm Shed is a pocket electronics calculator. It does not collect, store or transmit any personal data, and it has no way to: the app has no network access, requests no device permissions, shows no ads, and contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting libraries.

What stays on your device

Everything you type into a calculator exists only on your device while you use it. Any preference the app remembers, such as a default voltage reference, is stored on the device only and is removed when you uninstall the app.

What we receive

Nothing. We cannot see what you calculate, who you are, or whether you use the app at all. Install and crash statistics that Google Play or the App Store show developers are aggregated by the store under its own privacy policy, not by us.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If a future version ever needs a permission or sends data anywhere, this page will say exactly what and why before that version ships.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the developer contact on the app's store listing.
